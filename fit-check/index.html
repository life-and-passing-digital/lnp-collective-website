Straight answers

No spin, no sales deck. Just what you need to know before we collaborate further.

Start by submitting a high-level project scope from our online form here or from the header. We respond and connect with you within the first 24 hours. We will book an initial 30 mins discovery call to understand your business and needs in more detail before taking things further.

We start with a clear Statement of Work (SOW) that defines scope, deliverables, timelines, talent requirements, and what success looks like. Once agreed, pricing is straightforward and tied directly to that scope of work.

For most work, we price fixed-fee per phase so costs are known upfront. For ongoing or evolving work, we use a monthly retainer based on agreed capacity.

We’re also open to introducing a simple rate card for transparency on how pricing is built. That can sit underneath the SOW as a reference point, but we still prefer to quote the full project or phase so there’s no need to track hours or manage billing complexity.

Both; We work with both startups and established businesses at every stage of growth.

For startups: we help turn early ideas into validated products and launch-ready MVPs.

For scale-ups and enterprise teams: we support digital transformation, product innovation, and platform modernisation. Our approach adapts to the maturity and needs of your business.

What stays the same is a senior-led delivery. The people you speak to are the people doing the work. We bring deep expertise across strategy, design, engineering, AI and product delivery. This leads to faster decisions, higher-quality outcomes, and fewer layers between idea and execution.

Project duration largely depends on the complexity of your requirements. On average, most MVPs with a lean product offering could be delivered within 8-12 weeks.

Large platforms are delivered in phases, with working software released in regular iterations so you can validate ideas, gather feedback, and realise value early.

Throughout the engagement, we maintain transparency by:

  • Define a clear Statement of Work (SOW) with agreed deliverables, milestones, and timelines.
  • Running collaborative workshops to prioritise your product roadmap using the Desirability, Viability, and Feasibility (DVF) framework.
  • Delivering work in sprint cycles with regular ceremonies including stand-ups, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, and retrospectives.
  • Provide regular SteerCo updates with visibility into progress, key decisions, risks, dependencies and upcoming priorities.

Every repository, design file, and account belongs to you from day one. At the end of the engagement, we hand over any documentation and files for your teams to continue using. No vendor lock-in and no hidden licensing agreements.
